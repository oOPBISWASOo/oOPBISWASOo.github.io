About

I am a PhD candidate at the Nicolaus Copernicus Astronomical Center of the Polish Academy of Sciences (CAMK PAN) in Warsaw, working with Prof. Agata Różańska. My PhD is funded by an NCN OPUS grant.

02

Research

I study warm coronae in the accretion disks of active galactic nuclei (AGN). I model these systems with radiative transfer codes combined with relativistic ray tracing, in a framework we call TiNoGyo.
