About

I am a PhD candidate at CAMK PAN in Warsaw, working with Prof. Agata Różańska. My PhD is funded by an NCN OPUS grant.

Research

I study warm coronae in the accretion disks of active galactic nuclei (AGN). I model these systems with the radiative transfer codes TITAN and NOAR, combined with relativistic ray tracing through GYOTO, in a framework we call TiNoGyo.
